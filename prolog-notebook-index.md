# Prolog Notebook

Jupyter-style notebooks for Prolog, running SWI-Prolog compiled to WebAssembly — in
your browser, with nothing installed and nothing sent anywhere.

The chapter below is a real one. Press **Run**, then **`; next`**, and watch the
solutions arrive one at a time, exactly as they would at an interpreter's prompt.
Edit the program and consult your own version; `reset` puts the chapter's back.
